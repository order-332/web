The Libraries

  • Our Zine and our Wiki. (both coming soon)
  • The-Eye is a community-run archive that keeps old websites, text files, manuals, software, audio and other forgotten digital odds and ends from disappearing for good.
  • The Anarchist Library is a reading room of political and philosophical texts, free to read, free to copy.
  • The Book of Secret Knowledge is a big pile of lists, one-liners, cheat sheets, write-ups and command line tricks.
  • The Imperial Library is a shelf of DRM-free books you can actually keep.

News

  • The EFF the people fighting for civil liberties online, in courts and in code.
  • Hak5 is a long-running show about hardware hacking, gadgets and whatever broke this week.
  • Hacker News is a news board where programmers and founders argue in the comments.
  • The Hacker News is daily coverage of breaches, bugs, patches and where the whole field is heading.

Privacy & Anonymity

  • Tor Project is an anonymity network that bounces your traffic around so nobody can easily see where it came from or where it went.
  • Mullvad an open-source VPN that doesn't want your name.
  • Surveillance Self-Defense is a set of guides, tools and how-tos for keeping your communications private.
  • Tips on Remaining Anonymous notes from people who do this for a living.
  • How to Exit the Matrix
  • Privacy Tools is a list of services and software that push back against mass surveillance.
  • ProtonMail is an end-to-end encrypted email service.
  • Guerrilla Mail is a throwaway inbox for sign-ups you don't trust.
  • Veracrypt is free disk encryption that runs on Windows, Mac and Linux. (RIP the old one)
  • Qubes OS is an operating system built around keeping everything in its own little box.
  • PRISM Break is a directory of decentralized and self-hosted replacements for the big platforms.
  • SecureDrop is a platform that lets sources talk to journalists without getting burned.

Security

  • Security Education Companion is free material for people who teach digital security to others.
  • Linux Security is a guide by Linuxtopia on locking down a Linux box.
  • Hack This Site! is a free, legal training ground for hackers, built around challenges.
  • 332 CTF is our capture the flag. A legal place to break things, learn something and test what you think you know.
  • Cybrary is an online school for security and IT skills.

Linux

  • Linux From Scratch (LFS) is a project that walks you through building your own Linux system from source, one package at a time.
  • Linux Journey is a free course for people who have never opened a terminal.
  • linux Newbie Guide is a gentle starting point if Linux is brand new to you.
  • Linuxtopia is a deep reference for anyone learning or running open source in the real world.

Neat Sites

  • 332 circuit is the crew etched into a circuit board.

  • 332 orbit is a portal of planets on the wire.
  • 332 wired is a program diary printed to a dithered desk.
  • 332 ff7 box is a menu window with the drop dates in it.
  • The Courage Foundation raises money and support for people who blew the whistle and paid for it.
  • WikiLeaks publishes leaked documents and keeps them up.
  • Spyware Watchdog reviews software by how much it spies on you.
  • ELDRITCHDATA a dark little corner of hacking, privacy and odd files.
  • Intrinsically Repulsive a personal site with strong opinions about tech and the web.
  • Dig Deeper long reads on privacy, browsers and getting off the big platforms.
  • 0x00sec a community forum for hackers, reversers and malware nerds.
  • textfiles.com is a site full of old text files and articles collected over the years.
  • Dark.fail is a status page that tracks which hidden services are up.
  • Riseup is a group that runs communication tools for people organizing for change. They build alternatives that don't need anybody's permission.
  • Districts is a directory of small handmade sites, so you can find more pages like this one.

Tech

  • Pwnagotchi is a little "AI" on a tiny board that learns from the WiFi around it and collects handshakes while you carry it around (either by listening quietly or by being rude about it).
  • Bash Bunny is a USB stick that pretends to be a keyboard, an ethernet adapter, a serial port, or all of them at once.
  • The Wi-Fi Pineapple is a wireless auditing box that lets "network administrators" test their own networks.
  • The USB Rubber Ducky is a keyboard in a flash drive costume.
  • Project MF is a working simulation of in-band telephone signaling, the way the old network did it decades ago.
  • Tastic RFID Thief [Build Guide] is an RFID reader that reads badges from further than you'd like, hidden in something boring. [Build Guide]
  • Proxmark is a pocket knife for RFID, high and low frequency.
  • HackRF One is a software defined radio that can hear and speak across most of the spectrum. Open hardware, plugs into USB, runs standalone if you want.
  • Digispark USB Development Board is a tiny microcontroller board, cheaper and smaller than the usual one, only a bit weaker.
  • MagSpoof is a gadget that fakes a magnetic stripe out of thin air, "wirelessly".
  • Flipper Zero [Discord] is a toy-shaped multi-tool for radios, access control, hardware and more. Open source, endlessly hackable. [Discord]
  • WiFi Nugget is a small WiFi board with a screen and buttons, soldered and ready to flash.
